DAEX Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

DAEX runs its AI features directly on your device. This policy explains, plainly and specifically, what that does and doesn't mean for your data.

Summary

  • No account or sign-up is required to use DAEX.
  • Your conversations, saved prompts, memory, and uploaded documents are stored locally on your device and are never sent to any server operated by DAEX.
  • DAEX does not include any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Data Storage

Conversations, saved/pinned prompts, the app's internal memory of past interactions, and any documents you add to the offline knowledge base are stored in a local, on-device database. This data is never synced to any cloud service DAEX operates, and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its storage from Android's app settings.

Android's own operating system may include some of this local data in your device's built-in backup to your personal Google account, if that feature is enabled on your device (this is a standard Android OS capability, not something DAEX initiates or has separate access to).

Microphone & Voice Features

DAEX offers two distinct voice-input features, and they behave differently:

  • DAEX Live (continuous voice conversations): speech is transcribed using an on-device recognition engine bundled with the app. Audio for this feature is processed on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.
  • Quick voice-to-text (the microphone button in the message box): this feature uses your Android device's own built-in speech-recognition service to convert speech to text. Depending on your device and its settings, that system service may process this audio on-device or send it to its own provider's servers (typically Google) for recognition. This is governed by that service's own privacy policy, not DAEX's — DAEX does not operate, control, or receive a copy of this data.

Internet Access

DAEX requests internet access solely to:

  • Download AI model files (e.g. Gemma models) and voice-synthesis files from their original public sources, so they can run locally afterward;
  • Check for and install app updates.

Internet access is never used to transmit your conversations, documents, memory, or any other personal data to DAEX or any third party.

Documents You Add

Files you choose to add to the offline knowledge base (for example, PDFs or text notes) are read, indexed, and searched entirely on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere and remain under your control — you can remove them at any time from within the app.

On-Device Tools

DAEX can optionally use certain device capabilities on your behalf — for example, checking battery or storage status, launching other apps, composing an email, or searching your own past conversations. Each of these is individually switchable in Settings, higher-risk ones are off by default, and all of them run locally on your device. DAEX does not transmit the results of these actions anywhere.

Crash Reports

If DAEX crashes, a diagnostic log is saved locally on your device only. Sharing that log with the developer is entirely optional and requires you to take a deliberate action (such as attaching it to an email) — it is never collected or sent automatically.

Third-Party Services

DAEX does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only third parties involved at all are (a) the original providers of AI model files you choose to download (e.g. Google, for Gemma models), and (b) your device's own built-in speech-recognition service for the quick voice-to-text feature described above.

Children's Privacy

DAEX is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from anyone in that age group.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how DAEX handles data can be sent to contact@diizzy.dev.
